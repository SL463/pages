Troubleshooting notes · Arc Audio Black 1.0 · Sep 29, 2026

My right tweeter sounds off

It sounds wrong, but its frequency response measures about the same as the left one. I measured both tweeters to find out what's different.

Bottom line

  1. Both tweeters show similar SPL responses.
  2. The right tweeter has much higher distortion: typically 3.75% vs. 0.06% for the left, above the crossover.
  3. The right tweeter is missing the impedance peak the left one shows near 950 Hz.

Setup

Signal chain
Helix DSP Ultra → Helix C-FOUR → 99 µF cap → tweeter
Crossover
3.5 kHz high-pass, Linkwitz-Riley 24 dB/oct, no EQ
Amp gain
Set with an oscilloscope, with extra headroom
Mounting
A-pillars. The 99 µF cap has been in place since day one.
